CHAKRAVYUH — PROJECT STRUCTURE
===============================================================================
Root Directory: web-security-awareness-portal/
A dual-architecture cybersecurity awareness web portal featuring a Flask backend (with Jinja2 templates, SQLite, SQLAlchemy, Flask-Login, and Gemini AI) and a modern React 19 + Vite frontend (with TailwindCSS and Framer Motion).
