How to Report Cybercrime in India

Follow these steps to file a complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Important: File your complaint within 24-48 hours. The sooner you report, the higher the chances of recovery or action. Always keep digital evidence intact.

Open your web browser and navigate to the official portal: cybercrime.gov.in. Ensure the URL is exactly correct to avoid phishing sites.

On the homepage, locate and click the "Report Cyber Crime" button, or "Report Women/Child Related Crime" if applicable. Read the terms and accept them to proceed.

Register using your mobile number and state. You will receive an OTP. Once logged in, select the appropriate category such as Financial Fraud, Women/Child, or Other Cybercrime.

Provide accurate details of the incident: date, time, platform where it occurred, and a detailed description of what happened. Be clear and concise.

Upload relevant evidence. This includes screenshots of chats, emails, malicious URLs, bank statements showing unauthorized transactions, transaction IDs, or any other digital footprint left by the attacker.

Review your complaint and submit it. You will receive an acknowledgment number on your registered mobile number and email. Save this number securely.

Use the acknowledgment number to track the status of your complaint on the portal. For severe cases, it is highly recommended to also visit your local police station to file an official FIR (First Information Report) referencing your online complaint.
Checklist for Evidence
  • Bank/wallet transaction IDs
  • Screenshots of emails/SMS
  • URLs of fraudulent websites
  • Headers of phishing emails
  • Contact numbers of the fraudster
