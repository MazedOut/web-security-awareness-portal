Indian Cyber Threat Feed

Latest trending cyber scams and threats targeting Indian users.

FedEx/Courier Fraud
Critical

Ongoing

How it works: Victims receive a fake call claiming to be from FedEx or Customs stating a parcel in their name has been intercepted with illegal drugs. The caller transfers them to fake "police" who extort money via Skype/WhatsApp video calls under the guise of verifying bank accounts.

Prevention: Disconnect the call immediately. Law enforcement never asks for money transfers for "verification" or conducts official business via WhatsApp/Skype.

Sextortion via Video Call
Critical

Ongoing

How it works: Victim accepts a WhatsApp video call from an unknown number. The screen shows a nude video, and the caller secretly records the victim's face alongside it. They then blackmail the victim, threatening to share the morphed video with friends and family.

Prevention: Never answer video calls from unknown numbers. If you do and see something suspicious, cover the camera immediately and disconnect.

OTP Forwarding Scam
High

Recent

How it works: Scammer calls pretending to be from a telecom operator offering 5G upgrades. They ask the victim to dial a specific USSD code (like *401*number#). This enables call forwarding, allowing the scammer to intercept OTP calls to bypass 2FA.

Prevention: Never dial numbers or USSD codes provided by unknown callers. Telecom companies do not ask you to dial codes for upgrades.

UPI Collect Request Fraud
High

Persistent

How it works: While selling items on platforms like OLX, the "buyer" says they will send money via UPI. Instead, they send a UPI "Collect Request". The victim enters their UPI PIN thinking they are receiving money, but the amount is actually deducted from their account.

Prevention: Remember: UPI PIN is ONLY required for SENDING money, never for receiving money.

Job Offer / Task Fraud
High

Trending

How it works: Victims get messages offering part-time work (e.g., liking YouTube videos). They earn small initial amounts, gaining trust. They are then asked to "invest" in prepaid tasks for higher returns, eventually losing large sums when the scammers disappear.

Prevention: Genuine employers never ask you to pay or "invest" money to do a job.

KYC Expiry Phishing
High

Persistent

How it works: Fake SMS claiming your Bank/PAN/SIM KYC has expired and services will be blocked in 24 hours. Contains a malicious link to a fake portal to harvest net banking credentials and OTPs.

Prevention: Never click links in panic SMS. Verify with your bank directly using official apps or websites.

WhatsApp .zip Malware
Medium

Emerging

How it works: Malicious APKs disguised as document .zip files or app updates (like "Pink WhatsApp") are circulated in WhatsApp groups. Installing them compromises the phone, allowing hackers to read SMS and steal credentials.

Prevention: Only install apps from the official Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Do not install APKs from WhatsApp.

Aadhaar/PAN Identity Theft
Medium

Ongoing

How it works: Scammers use leaked Aadhaar or PAN details to open fake bank accounts, take out loans, or procure SIM cards under the victim's name.

Prevention: Use the mAadhaar app to lock your biometrics. Regularly check your CIBIL score for unknown loans. Use masked Aadhaar where full Aadhaar is not mandatory.

QR Code Payment Scam
Medium

Persistent

How it works: Fraudster sends a QR code via WhatsApp to a seller, claiming scanning it will deposit money into their account. Scanning it actually deducts money.

Prevention: Scanning a QR code ALWAYS deducts money from your account. You do not need to scan a QR code to receive money.

CERT-In Advisory: Critical Vulnerabilities
Advisory

Weekly Update

Summary: CERT-In regularly issues advisories regarding critical vulnerabilities in popular software (Chrome, Android, Windows, Cisco products).

Prevention: Keep your OS, browsers, and all applications updated to the latest versions automatically to patch these vulnerabilities.
